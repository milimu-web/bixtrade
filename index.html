How it works

Three steps from market to result

No order books and no leverage. You trade one direction on one market for a fixed time.

Choose a market and a stake

Pick a currency pair or a metal. Set your stake between $5 and $100 and a duration of 30, 60 or 120 seconds.

Call the direction

Press BUY if you think the price will be higher when the timer ends, or SELL if you think it will be lower. Your entry price is locked when you place the trade.

The price at expiry decides

When the timer ends, the price is compared with your entry price. A win pays 70% of your stake on top of your stake back. A loss costs your stake.

Rules & payout

One rule decides every trade

Real and demo trades settle the same way. The result depends only on your entry price, the price at expiry and the direction you chose.

Questions

Straight answers

Are the prices real market prices?

No. Brixtrade prices are simulated by the platform and are not live market quotes. The chart and the settlement both use the same simulated price feed, and the app labels it as simulated.

How is a trade decided?

By the price at expiry compared with your entry price. BUY wins if the price is higher, SELL wins if it is lower, and an equal price returns your stake. This is the same for real and demo trades.

What do I earn if I win, and what do I lose?

A win pays 70% of your stake. A $10 stake returns $17, which is your $10 back plus $7. A loss costs the whole stake. You need to win about 58.8% of your trades to break even.

Can I lose more than my stake?

No. The most you can lose on a trade is the stake you placed, and stakes are limited to $100.

Can I try it without money?

Yes. Demo mode gives you $1,000 of virtual money and uses the same rules as real trades.

How do I deposit and withdraw?

From your Profile / Wallet page with M-PESA. The deposit rate is $1 = KES 130, with a $1 minimum deposit and a $2 minimum withdrawal.

How do I stop myself from losing too much?

Set a daily loss limit in the trading room. When your real-money loss for the day reaches it, trading pauses until tomorrow. You can lower the limit at any time, but you cannot raise or remove it after you have reached it that day.

Who can use Brixtrade?

You must be 18 or older. Check that this kind of trading is allowed where you live before you deposit.

Risk warning

Trading is risky and most people lose money over time. Only trade money you can afford to lose. At a 70% payout you must win about 58.8% of trades just to break even, and past results do not predict future results.
